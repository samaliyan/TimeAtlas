# Changelog

## 1.0 — 2026-11-17

First release.

- World clock for the cities you choose, with time differences from your home city.
- Convert any time: tap a city to pick a time there, drag the ruler, or pick a date — every city updates at once.
- Daylight-saving time is always calculated for the chosen date; impossible or repeated times are explained.
- Search 400+ cities (and every other time zone of the phone) by city, country, state or abbreviation (PST, CET, IRST…). Works offline.
- Home-screen widget: up to 4 cities, light / dark / auto / clear styles, rows or columns depending on its size.
- Share the converted times as text.
- 12/24-hour and light/dark theme follow the phone, or can be set in the app.
- Checks GitHub for new versions when the app opens and installs them from inside the app.
